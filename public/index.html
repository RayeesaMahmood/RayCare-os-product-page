FAQ

Questions hospital teams ask

Do we have to replace our HIS or billing system?

No. RayCare runs beside your HIS. Start standalone on day one, then send events to your HIS or any integration tool through signed webhooks.

What hardware do we need?

One small server or office PC on your network, or a cloud VM. Any smart TV runs the lobby display in its browser, staff use any browser, and a 58 mm thermal printer is optional for token slips.

How are wait times calculated?

For each room, RayCare combines the patients ahead, their priority and the doctor's real consult pace today. Once five consults are complete, estimates follow the actual pace rather than the configured average.

What about patients without smartphones?

They get a printed slip, SMS messages work on basic phones, and spoken announcements call each token in the patient's language.

How do SMS and WhatsApp messages get sent?

RayCare posts each message to a webhook you choose, signed so your gateway can verify it. That keeps you free to use any SMS or WhatsApp provider your hospital already pays for.

Does the AI Copilot see patient data?

Copilot is optional and off unless an administrator adds an API key. It works from the live operations snapshot and every action it takes runs with the signed-in user's permissions and appears in the audit log.

How do we back up?

All data lives in one database file. Run npm run backup on a schedule, or copy the data folder with your existing backup tool.
